# Business Questions

In this phase, we should have defined the business questions that we want to answer. The goal is to have a clear understanding of the business objectives and the questions that we want to answer.

### Sales

- How much revenur does the business generate?
- How does revenue change over time?
- How much revenue does each country generate?
- How much revenue does each customer segment generate?
- What is AOV?

### Customers

- How many customers does the business have?
- Which countries have the most customers?
- Who are the highest-value customers?
- Who are the most profitable customers?
- How many customers are repeat purchasers?
- Which customer segment generate the most revenue?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?

### Operations

- What is the average delivery time?
- Which shipping methods are most used?
- Are there delays?
- How many returns do we have?

These are some questions that we need to answer using the data. We should have a clear understanding of the business objectives and the questions that we want to answer.
